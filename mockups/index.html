performance_plan.md

How this reaches 100 on PageSpeed

No framework weight

Bootstrap, jQuery, Popper, Font Awesome, AOS and Unite Gallery are replaced by one ~25 KB stylesheet, inline SVG icons, ~1 KB of vanilla JS, a native popover menu and a native dialog lightbox.

Fonts & images

One self-hosted variable font (41 KB, preloaded, metric-matched fallback) and a mono subset (22 KB). Artwork served as sized WebP with explicit dimensions; gallery originals (≈66 MB) are resized once at build time.

Same SEO & meta

The head meta block, {% seo %}, manifest, theme-color, icons and RSS link are carried over verbatim. Analytics, Disqus, Pageclip and Razorpay load deferred.
